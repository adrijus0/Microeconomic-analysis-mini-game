Common ratio effect: a Minecraft expedition

Two plans for getting diamonds

$P_A$: villager trade, 30 diamonds for sure.
$P_B$: deep cave, 40 diamonds w.p. 0.8 (a creeper w.p. 0.2).

Same plans, but you first need to find a stronghold (w.p. $q=0.25$):
$P_C$: 30 w.p. 0.25  $P_D$: 40 w.p. $0.25\times0.8=0.20$.

  • $x_H=40$, $x_M=30$, $x_0=0$, $p=0.8$, $q=0.25$.
  • Typical: $P_A\succ P_B$ (“sure thing!”) but $P_D\succ P_C$ (“20% vs 25%, go for 40”).
Scaling all winning chances by $q$ should not change the choice — but it does.

Ellsberg paradox: the Khajiit’s sack of gems

A merchant’s sack holds 90 gems

30 rubies; 60 emeralds or sapphires in an unknown mix. Draw one blindly; win 100 gold if your bet is right.

Typical choice
I $\succ$ II (“I know it is 1/3”) and IV $\succ$ III (“I know it is 2/3”).

Why no beliefs can explain it

Let $\pi_R=\tfrac13$, $\pi_E$, $\pi_S$ be the player’s subjective probabilities, $\pi_E+\pi_S=\tfrac23$.

$$\begin{align*} \text{I}\succ\text{II} &\iff \pi_R\,u(100) > \pi_E\,u(100) \iff \pi_R>\pi_E,\\ \text{IV}\succ\text{III} &\iff (\pi_E+\pi_S)\,u(100) > (\pi_R+\pi_S)\,u(100) \iff \pi_E>\pi_R . \end{align*}$$
  • Contradiction for every belief and every $u$.
  • “Sapphire” is a common consequence $\Rightarrow$ Savage’s sure-thing principle fails.

Framing: Whiterun under dragon attack

A dragon will kill 600 citizens unless the Jarl acts

Frame 1 (lives saved):

  • Plan A: 200 people will be saved.[72%]
  • Plan B: 1/3 chance 600 saved, 2/3 chance nobody saved.[28%]

Frame 2 (deaths):

  • Plan C: 400 people will die.[22%]
  • Plan D: 1/3 chance nobody dies, 2/3 chance 600 die.[78%]
Paradox (Tversky & Kahneman, 1981; Lecture 5)
A $\equiv$ C and B $\equiv$ D. Only the words changed, yet the majority switches: risk-averse for gains, risk-seeking for losses.

Loss aversion and small bets: hardcore mode

A 50–50 coin bet in a hardcore world

Lose 1 000 emeralds or win 1 050 emeralds. Do you accept?

  • Most say no, although the expected value is $+25$.
  • Rabin (2000): rejecting it at every wealth level forces EU to reject a 50–50 bet: lose 20 000 or win any amount. Absurd.
Explanation: losses hurt more than gains please
With $\lambda\approx2.25$: $0.5\cdot1050-0.5\cdot2.25\cdot1000=-600<0$ $\Rightarrow$ reject.
Losing your inventory hurts more than finding the same loot.

Prospect theory (Kahneman & Tversky)

$$V(L)=\sum_i w(p_i)\,v(x_i),\quad v(x)=\begin{cases}x^{\alpha}, & x\ge0\\ -\lambda(-x)^{\beta}, & x<0\end{cases},\quad w(p)=\frac{p^{\gamma}}{\big(p^{\gamma}+(1-p)^{\gamma}\big)^{1/\gamma}}$$

$x$ = gain/loss vs. a reference point; $\alpha=\beta=0.88$, $\lambda=2.25$, $\gamma=0.61$.

concave for gains, convex and steeper for losses

small $p$ overweighted, large $p$ underweighted
